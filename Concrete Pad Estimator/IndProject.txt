Concrete Pad Estimator

Scenario: Your close friend Jim, recently inherited his uncle's construction business, which specializes in 
building large warehouse facilities. While Jim is confident in his construction skills, he quickly realizes 
that he lacks experience in the business and financial aspects of running the company. In particular, he 
finds the process of estimating concrete pad projects—including materials, labor, and costs—both confusing 
and frustrating.

To help manage this challenge, Jim has asked you, a friend with programming experience and some knowledge 
of concrete work, to develop a Concrete Pad Estimating Application. This program will assist him in 
generating accurate estimates for concrete pad jobs and improving overall business efficiency.

Project Objective
Design and develop a Java-based application that calculates and generates estimates specifically for concrete 
pad construction projects.

Core Features
Your application must include the following:

Initial Estimate Input
Allow the user to input basic project details (project name, location, etc.).
Size of Work Area
Accept and calculate total area (length × width in feet).
Concrete Requirements
Calculate the volume of concrete needed based on slab thickness.
Labor Estimation
Input number of employees
Estimate total labor hours
Calculate labor cost
Cost Calculations
Concrete material cost
Labor cost
Total project cost

Technical Requirements

Implement CSV file integration to store and retrieve:
Manpower hours
Work hours
Ensure all calculations are accurate and clearly presented to the user.

Additional Features
Consider adding the following enhancements:

Improved Estimation Tools
Pre-set slab types (e.g., residential pad, warehouse slab)
Variable thickness options
Waste percentage calculations
Discount System
Apply percentage or fixed discounts to the final estimate
